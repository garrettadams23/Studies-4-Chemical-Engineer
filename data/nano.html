🔬 Nanoscale Phenomena & Synthesis FOUNDATION ▶
Small Is Different
At the nanoscale, surface beats bulk
Below ~100 nm, the surface-to-volume ratio explodes and quantum effects appear — materials gain properties their bulk form never had. Chemical engineers make and process these materials at scale.
▸ WHY NANO IS DIFFERENT
EffectConsequence
Huge surface areaCatalysis, adsorption, reactivity soar
Quantum confinementTunable optical/electronic properties (quantum dots)
Dominant surface energyLower melting points, self-assembly
▸ SYNTHESIS ROUTES
Bottom-up:  sol-gel · CVD · self-assembly · precipitation
Top-down:   milling · lithography

Bottom-up gives finer control; top-down scales more easily.
⚗️ Nanoparticle catalysis links straight back to the Reaction Engineering domain — more surface per gram means more active sites.
🧿 Nano Characterization & Applications APPLIED ▶
Seeing & Using the Nanoscale
From electron microscopes to drug delivery
Nanomaterials are characterized by specialized microscopy and scattering, and applied across catalysis, medicine, energy and electronics.
▸ CHARACTERIZATION TOOLS
ToolReveals

▸ APPLICATIONS
Catalysis (Pt nanoparticles) · membranes · quantum dots
Targeted drug delivery · nanocomposites · batteries & solar
⚠️ Nanomaterial safety (inhalation, environmental fate) is an active concern — process safety and toxicology extend to the nanoscale.
⚠️ Nanomaterial Safety & Environment RESPONSIBLE ▶
Small Particles, New Risks
The same size that gives power raises concern
The reactivity and mobility that make nanomaterials useful also raise health and environmental questions. Responsible development handles them with the same rigor as any process hazard.
▸ WHY NANO NEEDS CARE
ConcernReason
InhalationNanoparticles reach deep lung / cross membranes
High reactivityLarge surface area → catalytic / oxidative activity
Environmental fateMobility & persistence not fully understood
▸ CONTROLS
Containment & ventilation · appropriate PPE (respirators)
Handle as fine powders (dust-explosion + toxicity aware)
Lifecycle thinking: exposure at synthesis, use, and disposal.
🦺 Nano safety extends Process Safety & industrial hygiene to a new length scale — precaution while the toxicology matures.
🔋 Nanomaterials in Energy & Medicine APPLICATIONS ▶
Where Nano Pays Off
Batteries, catalysts and targeted drugs
Nanomaterials' huge surface area and tunable properties make them transformative in energy storage, catalysis, and medicine — areas chemical engineers scale up.
▸ FLAGSHIP APPLICATIONS
FieldNano role
BatteriesNanostructured electrodes → faster charge, more capacity
CatalysisHigh surface area → more active sites per gram
Drug deliveryNanoparticles target & release drugs (e.g. LNP mRNA)
MembranesNanoporous films for selective separation
💉 Lipid nanoparticles delivering mRNA vaccines (see Biochemical) are a headline nano- medicine success — formulation engineering at the nanoscale.
