📈 Statistical Process Control & Monitoring FOUNDATION ▶
Is the Process In Control?
Separating signal from noise in plant data
Every measurement varies. Statistical process control distinguishes normal common-cause variation from a real, assignable shift that demands action.
▸ CONTROL CHARTS
Center line = process mean
Control limits = mean ± 3σ   (≈ 99.7% of in-control data)

Out-of-control signals: point beyond 3σ, runs, trends,
increasing spread → investigate an assignable cause.
▸ MULTIVARIATE METHODS
MethodDoes
PCACompresses many correlated sensors to a few components
PLSRelates process variables to quality outputs
CpkProcess capability vs. spec limits
🎯 Don't tamper: reacting to common-cause noise as if it were a real shift ("overcontrol") actually increases variability — a lesson from Deming's funnel experiment.
🤖 Machine Learning & Digital Twins MODERN ▶
Data-Driven Process Engineering
Models learned from data, not just first principles
When first-principles models are too slow or incomplete, ML learns behavior from plant data — powering soft sensors, digital twins, and real-time optimization.
▸ WHERE ML HELPS
ApplicationIdea
Soft sensorPredict a hard-to-measure quality from easy sensors
Surrogate modelFast approximation of a slow simulation
Digital twinLive model mirroring the real unit for what-if runs
Predictive maintenanceFlag failures from vibration/thermal patterns
▸ THE WORKFLOW
Data (historian) → clean & feature-engineer → train/validate
→ deploy → monitor for drift → retrain

Python stack: pandas, scikit-learn, NumPy — plus domain physics
(physics-informed ML beats black boxes on extrapolation).
🧠 Garbage in, garbage out applies double: a model is only as good as its data and only trustworthy inside the range it was trained on.
🎯 Real-Time Optimization & Advanced Control OPERATIONS ▶
Squeezing Out the Last Percent
Models that steer the plant to its economic optimum
Beyond keeping variables at setpoint, modern plants continuously push toward the most profitable operating point using layered optimization and model-predictive control.
▸ THE AUTOMATION PYRAMID
Planning & scheduling (days)
  ↑
Real-Time Optimization — RTO (hours): economic optimum
  ↑
Model Predictive Control — MPC (minutes): multivariable, constrained
  ↑
Regulatory (PID) control (seconds): hold setpoints
▸ WHY MPC WINS
FeatureBenefit
Handles interactionsCoordinates many coupled loops
Respects constraintsPushes against limits safely
Uses a modelPredicts & acts before deviations grow
💰 Operating closer to constraints is where the money is — MPC safely runs a plant nearer its true limits than a human or plain PID could.
🧹 Data Cleaning & Feature Engineering FOUNDATION ▶
80% of the Work
Good models need good data first
Plant historian data is messy — noise, outliers, gaps, drift. Cleaning and engineering features is where most of a data project's effort (and value) lives.
▸ COMMON DATA PROBLEMS
IssueHandling
Outliers / spikesFilter, robust stats, sanity limits
Missing valuesInterpolate, forward-fill, or drop
Time alignmentResample sensors to a common clock; account for lag
Steady-state IDModel on steady data, exclude transients
▸ FEATURE ENGINEERING
Derive physically meaningful features:
  ratios, dimensionless groups, rolling averages, lags,
  first principles (e.g. compute a driving force, not raw T's).
Domain knowledge beats brute force.
🧠 Engineering a feature from first principles (a known dimensionless group) usually outperforms letting a model rediscover physics from noisy raw signals.
🔧 Predictive Maintenance & Anomaly Detection RELIABILITY ▶
Fix It Just Before It Breaks
Data catching failures early
Unplanned equipment failure is costly and dangerous. Sensor data plus models predict failures before they happen — shifting from reactive to condition-based maintenance.
▸ MAINTENANCE STRATEGIES
StrategyTrigger
ReactiveRun to failure (worst for critical kit)
PreventiveFixed schedule (may over- or under-maintain)
PredictiveCondition-based, data-driven (optimal)
▸ HOW ANOMALIES ARE FOUND
Baseline "healthy" behavior (PCA, autoencoder, control limits)
→ flag deviations (vibration, temperature, current signatures)
→ estimate remaining useful life → schedule the fix.
🎛️ Predictive maintenance is statistical process control (see the SPC topic) applied to equipment health instead of product quality.
