🔌 Electrode Kinetics & Butler–Volmer FOUNDATION ▶
Reactions That Move Electrons
Overpotential drives the current
At an electrode, reaction rate is current. Driving the electrode away from its equilibrium potential — the overpotential η — speeds the reaction, described by the Butler–Volmer equation.
▸ BUTLER–VOLMER & TAFEL
i = i₀ [ exp(α_a F η / RT) − exp(−α_c F η / RT) ]

i₀ = exchange current density (intrinsic activity)
η  = E − E_eq (overpotential)

Large η → Tafel form:  η = a + b·log|i|   (straight line)
▸ SOURCES OF OVERPOTENTIAL
TypeOrigin
ActivationCharge-transfer barrier (Butler–Volmer)
Ohmic (IR)Electrolyte + contact resistance
ConcentrationReactant depletion at the surface (mass transfer)
⚡ A good catalyst raises i₀, shrinking the activation overpotential — why platinum-group catalysts dominate fuel cells and electrolyzers.
🔋 Batteries & Fuel Cells ENERGY STORAGE ▶
Storing & Converting Energy
Galvanic cells that power the transition
Batteries store energy chemically; fuel cells convert a continuously-fed fuel directly to electricity. Both are galvanic cells governed by the same thermodynamics.
▸ EFFICIENCY & VOLTAGE
Ideal (thermodynamic) efficiency  = ΔG / ΔH
Cell voltage  E = E° − (RT/nF) ln Q   (Nernst)
Actual voltage = E − η_activation − η_ohmic − η_concentration
▸ TECHNOLOGY MAP

🔬 Fuel cells dodge the Carnot limit because they convert chemical energy directly — not via heat — so their ideal efficiency (ΔG/ΔH) can exceed a heat engine's.
💧 Electrolysis & Green Hydrogen DECARBONIZATION ▶
Splitting Water with Electrons
The reverse of a fuel cell
Electrolysis uses electricity to drive a non-spontaneous reaction. Powered by renewables, water electrolysis makes green hydrogen — a linchpin of hard-to-electrify decarbonization.
▸ WATER ELECTROLYSIS
Cathode:  2H₂O + 2e⁻ → H₂ + 2OH⁻
Anode:    2OH⁻ → ½O₂ + H₂O + 2e⁻
Overall:  H₂O → H₂ + ½O₂     E°_cell = −1.23 V (needs work)

Energy: Faraday's law → mass ∝ (current × time)/(nF)
▸ THE "HYDROGEN COLORS"
ColorSource
GreenElectrolysis from renewable power (zero-carbon)
BlueSteam methane reforming + carbon capture
GreySteam methane reforming, CO₂ vented
🌱 Electrolyzer types mirror fuel cells (PEM, alkaline, solid-oxide) — the same hardware run in reverse. CO₂ electroreduction extends the idea to fuels and chemicals.
🥇 Electroplating & Electrowinning APPLIED ▶
Depositing Metal with Electrons
Faraday's law sets how much metal you get
Passing current through a metal-salt solution deposits metal on the cathode — for coatings (electroplating) or to recover metal from solution (electrowinning).
▸ FARADAY'S LAW OF ELECTROLYSIS
m = (Q · M) / (n · F) = (I · t · M) / (n · F)

m = mass deposited   Q = charge = I·t   M = molar mass
n = electrons/ion    F = 96 485 C/mol

Current efficiency < 100% (side reactions, e.g. H₂ evolution).
▸ USES
ProcessPurpose
ElectroplatingCorrosion/wear coatings, decorative finish
ElectrowinningRecover Cu, Zn, Ni, Au from leach solutions
ElectrorefiningPurify metals to high grade
⚡ Same Faraday relation as the electrolysis topic — charge in, moles of metal out. It's how most of the world's copper is finished.
