🌍 Carbon Capture, Utilization & Storage (CCUS) DECARBONIZATION ▶
Keeping CO₂ Out of the Air
Separation engineering aimed at the climate
CCUS applies classic separation unit operations — absorption, adsorption, membranes — to the world's largest waste stream: CO₂. It is a flagship application of chemical engineering to sustainability.
▸ CAPTURE ROUTES
RouteWhere CO₂ is caughtMethod
Post-combustionFrom flue gas after burningAmine absorption (MEA)
Pre-combustionFrom syngas before burningShift + physical solvent
Oxy-fuelBurn in O₂ → near-pure CO₂Air separation needed
Direct air captureFrom ambient air (~420 ppm)Sorbent / hydroxide loops
▸ THE AMINE LOOP
Absorber (cool):  CO₂ + amine → carbamate   (chemical absorption)
Stripper (hot):   heat regenerates the amine, releases pure CO₂
→ compress → pipeline → geological storage or utilization

The regeneration heat is the dominant energy cost.
♻️ It's the same absorption/stripping as gas sweetening (see Mass Transfer) — the engineering is mature; the challenge is doing it at gigatonne scale and low energy.
🚰 Water & Wastewater Treatment ENVIRONMENTAL ▶
Cleaning the Most Important Solvent
Physical, biological and chemical stages
Every plant and city runs water treatment. It chains unit operations to remove solids, organics and nutrients before discharge or reuse.
▸ TREATMENT STAGES
StageRemovesHow
PrimarySuspended solidsScreening, sedimentation
SecondaryDissolved organics (BOD)Activated sludge (microbes)
TertiaryNutrients (N, P), pathogensFiltration, membranes, UV/Cl₂
▸ KEY METRICS
BOD = biochemical oxygen demand (organic load)
COD = chemical oxygen demand
TSS = total suspended solids

Activated sludge is a live CSTR of microbes — a bioreactor
doing environmental work (see Biochemical & Reaction Eng).
💧 Reverse osmosis (see Mass Transfer) turns seawater and wastewater into drinking water — desalination is membrane engineering at civilization scale.
♻️ Life-Cycle Assessment & the Circular Economy SYSTEMS ▶
Counting the Full Footprint
Cradle-to-grave, then cradle-to-cradle
A process that looks clean at the gate may be dirty upstream. Life-cycle assessment tallies impacts across the whole value chain; the circular economy redesigns to eliminate waste entirely.
▸ THE FOUR LCA PHASES (ISO 14040)
1. Goal & scope   — define boundaries & functional unit
2. Inventory (LCI) — tally all inputs & emissions
3. Impact (LCIA)   — translate to GWP, acidification, etc.
4. Interpretation  — hotspots & improvement options
▸ GREEN-CHEMISTRY METRICS
MetricDefinition
Atom economyMW of product / Σ MW of reactants
E-factorkg waste / kg product
Carbon intensitykg CO₂e / kg product
🔁 Bulk chemicals have E-factors under 5; pharmaceuticals can exceed 100 — the higher the value, the more waste hides behind each kilogram of product.
💧 The Hydrogen Economy ENERGY TRANSITION ▶
A Clean Energy Carrier
Hydrogen for the hard-to-electrify sectors
Hydrogen can decarbonize steel, ammonia, refining and heavy transport where batteries fall short. Chemical engineers own its production, storage and use.
▸ PRODUCTION ROUTES
ColourRouteCarbon
GreySteam methane reformingCO₂ vented
BlueSMR + carbon captureLow
GreenElectrolysis + renewablesZero
▸ THE HARD PART — STORAGE
Low volumetric energy density → must compress (350–700 bar)
or liquefy (−253 °C, cryogenic) or bind as ammonia / LOHC.

Storage & transport, not production, is often the bottleneck.
🔗 Ties together Electrochemical (electrolysis), Cryogenics (liquefaction) and Petroleum (SMR) — a genuinely cross-domain engineering challenge.
