The Equations You Reach for Most
Every workhorse formula, one screen
A condensed pull-together of the most-used relations across the curriculum — for revision and exam day. Each links to its full treatment in the relevant domain.
▸ BALANCES & THERMODYNAMICS
General balance   In − Out + Gen − Cons = Accumulation
Species (extent)  n_i = n_i0 + ν_i ξ
Energy (open, SS) Q̇ − Ẇs = Σ ṁ_out h − Σ ṁ_in h
Ideal / real gas  PV = nRT ;  PV = ZnRT
Gibbs             ΔG = ΔH − TΔS ;  ΔG° = −RT ln K
Heat of reaction  ΔH°rxn = Σν ΔH°f(prod) − Σν ΔH°f(reac)
▸ TRANSPORT (MOMENTUM · HEAT · MASS)
Bernoulli   P/ρ + v²/2 + gz + w = const + h_f
Reynolds    Re = ρvD/μ
Darcy ΔP    ΔP = f (L/D)(ρv²/2)
Fourier     q = −kA dT/dx
Newton cool q = hA ΔT
Radiation   q = εσA(T₁⁴ − T₂⁴)
Exchanger   Q = U A F ΔT_lm
Fick        J = −D dC/dz
Column      Z = HTU × NTU
▸ REACTION & SEPARATIONS
Arrhenius   k = A exp(−E_a/RT)
CSTR        V = F_A0 X / (−r_A)
PFR         V = F_A0 ∫ dX/(−r_A)
Rel. vol.   α = (y_A/x_A)/(y_B/x_B)
Raoult      y_i P = x_i P_i^sat
Fenske      N_min = ln[(x_LK/x_HK)_D (x_HK/x_LK)_B]/ln α
📌 This is a map, not the territory — each formula's assumptions and derivation live in its home domain. Use it to jog memory, not to skip understanding.
