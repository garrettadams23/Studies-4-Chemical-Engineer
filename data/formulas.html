▸ TRANSFORMS & MODELS
L{1}=1/s   L{e^-at}=1/(s+a)   L{f'}=sF−f(0)   delay: e^(−θs)
First order:  G = K/(τs+1)     step reaches 63% at t=τ
Second order: G = K/(τ²s²+2ζτs+1)   ζ<1 underdamped (overshoot)
▸ CONTROLLERS & STABILITY
PID:  u = Kc[ e + (1/τ_I)∫e dt + τ_D de/dt ]
Stability: |GH|<1 at phase = −180°
Gain margin > ~2 (6 dB) · phase margin > ~30–45°
Valve: Q = Cv·sqrt(ΔP/SG)
🎯 P leaves offset; I removes it; D anticipates but amplifies noise. Most loops target ζ ≈ 0.4–0.7 for a fast, well-damped response.
