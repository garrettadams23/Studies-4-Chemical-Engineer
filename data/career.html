✍️ Technical Communication & Documentation PROFESSIONAL ▶
An Unshared Result Is a Wasted One
Turning analysis into decisions
Engineers are judged as much on communication as calculation. Clear reports, drawings and recommendations turn technical work into action — and protect you and the public.
▸ COMMUNICATION ESSENTIALS
FormKey rule
Report / memoLead with the conclusion & recommendation
Data & chartsShow uncertainty; label units & axes
P&IDs / drawingsStandard symbols; single source of truth
Safety docsPrecise, unambiguous, auditable
▸ WHY IT MATTERS
Decisions are made by people who won't read your derivation.
Document assumptions & basis — the next engineer (or an audit) needs them.
🗣️ "Bottom line up front": a decision-maker should get your recommendation in the first sentence, with the analysis available to back it up.
📊 Project Management & Execution DELIVERY ▶
Getting It Built, On Time & Budget
Stage-gates, scope, schedule & cost
Turning a design into an operating plant is a project. Engineers increasingly manage scope, schedule, cost and risk through a staged (front-end loading) process.
▸ STAGE-GATE (FEL) PROCESS
FEL-1 Appraise → FEL-2 Select → FEL-3 Define → Execute → Operate
Each gate: is it still worth funding? (kill bad projects early)
Cost-estimate accuracy tightens at each stage.
▸ THE PROJECT TRIANGLE
ConstraintReality
ScopeWhat's delivered — guard against creep
ScheduleCritical path sets the finish date
CostBudget + contingency for risk
🎯 Most cost overruns trace to poor early definition — front-end loading (getting FEL right) is the cheapest insurance against a runaway project.
🚀 Entrepreneurship & Innovation BEYOND THE PLANT ▶
Building New Things
From lab breakthrough to scaled business
Chemical engineers increasingly launch startups — in energy, materials, biotech and climate. The skill of scale-up translates directly into commercializing innovation.
▸ THE COMMERCIALIZATION VALLEY
Lab result → bench → pilot → demo → commercial
Each 10–100× scale-up is where many good ideas die
("the valley of death"): funding, scale-up risk, unit economics.
▸ WHAT MATTERS
FactorWhy
Unit economics$/kg at scale must beat incumbents
Scale-up riskBench success ≠ plant success
Techno-economicsTEA + LCA decide viability early
💡 Chemical engineers' scale-up expertise is exactly what deep-tech startups need — the "valley of death" is a scale-up and unit-economics problem you're trained to solve.
