💨 The Psychrometric Chart FOUNDATION ▶
Air + Water in One Diagram
Every drying, cooling & HVAC process lives here
The psychrometric chart plots the state of moist air. Reading it turns humidity balances (from the Material Balances domain) into quick graphical answers.
▸ CHART AXES & LINES
PropertyOn the chart
Dry-bulb THorizontal axis
Humidity ratioVertical axis
Relative humidityCurved lines (100% = saturation)
Wet-bulb / enthalpyDiagonal lines
▸ PROCESSES AS PATHS
Heating:        move right (constant humidity ratio)
Cooling+dehumid: down-left, following saturation then along it
Adiabatic sat:  along a constant wet-bulb line (evaporative cooling)
🌡️ Evaporative ("swamp") coolers ride a constant wet-bulb line — they cool by evaporating water, adding humidity as the temperature drops.
🏭 Cooling Towers & Air Conditioning APPLIED ▶
Rejecting Heat with Air & Water
Simultaneous heat and mass transfer
Cooling towers and air conditioners are simultaneous heat-and-mass-transfer devices — combining the Heat Transfer and Mass Transfer domains in one unit.
▸ COOLING-TOWER LIMITS
Water is cooled by evaporation into air.
Theoretical limit = the air's wet-bulb temperature.

Approach = T_water,out − T_wet-bulb   (smaller = bigger tower)
Range    = T_water,in − T_water,out
▸ KEY IDEAS
TermMeaning
Makeup waterReplaces evaporation + drift + blowdown
BlowdownPurge to limit dissolved-solids buildup
Latent loadDehumidification — the big AC energy cost
💧 A cooling tower is a purge problem too — evaporation concentrates salts, so blowdown controls cycles of concentration (echoing recycle/purge in Material Balances).
🌦️ Humidification & Dehumidification OPERATIONS ▶
Adding & Removing Water from Air
Gas–liquid contacting on the psychrometric chart
Many operations deliberately change air's moisture: humidifying dry air, or dehumidifying for comfort and process control. Each is a path on the psychrometric chart.
▸ THE OPERATIONS
OperationMethod
HumidificationContact air with water (spray, wetted wall)
Dehumidification (cooling)Cool below dew point → water condenses out
Dehumidification (sorption)Desiccant (silica gel, glycol) adsorbs water
▸ DRIVING FORCE
Water moves toward the phase where its partial pressure is lower.
Rate ∝ (interface humidity − bulk humidity) — a mass-transfer problem.
❄️ Air conditioning is mostly a dehumidification (latent) load — removing moisture, not just lowering temperature, dominates the energy bill.
🏢 HVAC Loads & Ventilation BUILDINGS & PLANTS ▶
Heating, Cooling & Fresh Air
Sensible + latent loads set the equipment
Conditioning a space is an energy and mass balance on air. The load splits into sensible (temperature) and latent (moisture) parts, plus fresh-air and safety ventilation.
▸ THE LOAD SPLIT
Sensible load  Q_s = ṁ_air · c_p · ΔT
Latent load    Q_L = ṁ_air · ΔH_ratio · λ (moisture removal)
Total = sensible + latent; the ratio sizes the coil & reheat.
▸ VENTILATION DRIVERS
NeedReason
Fresh airCO₂ & air quality for occupants
Hazard dilutionKeep flammable/toxic vapors below limits
PressurizationClean/hazard zones — control flow direction
🦺 In a plant, ventilation is a safety system — diluting leaks below the LFL and keeping control rooms at positive pressure (see Process Safety).
