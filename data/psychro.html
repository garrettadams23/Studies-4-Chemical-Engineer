💨 The Psychrometric Chart FOUNDATION ▶
Air + Water in One Diagram
Every drying, cooling & HVAC process lives here
The psychrometric chart plots the state of moist air. Reading it turns humidity balances (from the Material Balances domain) into quick graphical answers.
▸ CHART AXES & LINES
PropertyOn the chart
Dry-bulb THorizontal axis
Humidity ratioVertical axis
Relative humidityCurved lines (100% = saturation)
Wet-bulb / enthalpyDiagonal lines
▸ PROCESSES AS PATHS
Heating:        move right (constant humidity ratio)
Cooling+dehumid: down-left, following saturation then along it
Adiabatic sat:  along a constant wet-bulb line (evaporative cooling)
🌡️ Evaporative ("swamp") coolers ride a constant wet-bulb line — they cool by evaporating water, adding humidity as the temperature drops.
🏭 Cooling Towers & Air Conditioning APPLIED ▶
Rejecting Heat with Air & Water
Simultaneous heat and mass transfer
Cooling towers and air conditioners are simultaneous heat-and-mass-transfer devices — combining the Heat Transfer and Mass Transfer domains in one unit.
▸ COOLING-TOWER LIMITS
Water is cooled by evaporation into air.
Theoretical limit = the air's wet-bulb temperature.

Approach = T_water,out − T_wet-bulb   (smaller = bigger tower)
Range    = T_water,in − T_water,out
▸ KEY IDEAS
TermMeaning
Makeup waterReplaces evaporation + drift + blowdown
BlowdownPurge to limit dissolved-solids buildup
Latent loadDehumidification — the big AC energy cost
💧 A cooling tower is a purge problem too — evaporation concentrates salts, so blowdown controls cycles of concentration (echoing recycle/purge in Material Balances).
