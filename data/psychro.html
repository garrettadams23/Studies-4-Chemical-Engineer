🏭 Cooling Towers & Air Conditioning APPLIED ▶
Rejecting Heat with Air & Water
Simultaneous heat and mass transfer
Cooling towers and air conditioners are simultaneous heat-and-mass-transfer devices — combining the Heat Transfer and Mass Transfer domains in one unit.
▸ COOLING-TOWER LIMITS
Water is cooled by evaporation into air.
Theoretical limit = the air's wet-bulb temperature.

Approach = T_water,out − T_wet-bulb   (smaller = bigger tower)
Range    = T_water,in − T_water,out
▸ KEY IDEAS
TermMeaning
Makeup waterReplaces evaporation + drift + blowdown
BlowdownPurge to limit dissolved-solids buildup
Latent loadDehumidification — the big AC energy cost
💧 A cooling tower is a purge problem too — evaporation concentrates salts, so blowdown controls cycles of concentration (echoing recycle/purge in Material Balances).
🌦️ Humidification & Dehumidification OPERATIONS ▶
Adding & Removing Water from Air
Gas–liquid contacting on the psychrometric chart
Many operations deliberately change air's moisture: humidifying dry air, or dehumidifying for comfort and process control. Each is a path on the psychrometric chart.
▸ THE OPERATIONS
OperationMethod
HumidificationContact air with water (spray, wetted wall)
Dehumidification (cooling)Cool below dew point → water condenses out
Dehumidification (sorption)Desiccant (silica gel, glycol) adsorbs water
▸ DRIVING FORCE
Water moves toward the phase where its partial pressure is lower.
Rate ∝ (interface humidity − bulk humidity) — a mass-transfer problem.
❄️ Air conditioning is mostly a dehumidification (latent) load — removing moisture, not just lowering temperature, dominates the energy bill.
🏢 HVAC Loads & Ventilation BUILDINGS & PLANTS ▶
Heating, Cooling & Fresh Air
Sensible + latent loads set the equipment
Conditioning a space is an energy and mass balance on air. The load splits into sensible (temperature) and latent (moisture) parts, plus fresh-air and safety ventilation.
▸ THE LOAD SPLIT
Sensible load  Q_s = ṁ_air · c_p · ΔT
Latent load    Q_L = ṁ_air · ΔH_ratio · λ (moisture removal)
Total = sensible + latent; the ratio sizes the coil & reheat.
▸ VENTILATION DRIVERS
NeedReason
Fresh airCO₂ & air quality for occupants
Hazard dilutionKeep flammable/toxic vapors below limits
PressurizationClean/hazard zones — control flow direction
🦺 In a plant, ventilation is a safety system — diluting leaks below the LFL and keeping control rooms at positive pressure (see Process Safety).
💧 Humidity Definitions & Terms FOUNDATION ▶
Ways to Measure Water in Air
Humidity ratio, relative humidity & dew point
"Humidity" has several precise meanings. Mixing them up is a classic source of psychrometric errors — each one answers a different question.
▸ THE KEY QUANTITIES
Humidity ratio   W = 0.622·p_w /(P − p_w)   [kg water / kg dry air]
Relative humidity RH = p_w / p_ws × 100%     [how "full" the air is]
Dew point        T_dp: cool at constant P until RH = 100%
Specific humidity q = m_water /(m_water + m_dry air)
▸ WHY W (NOT RH) FOR BALANCES
QuantityBest for
Humidity ratio WMass balances — dry-air basis is conserved
Relative humidityComfort, condensation risk
Dew pointWhen will it condense? (constant-P cooling)

🌡️ Wet-Bulb & Adiabatic Saturation CORE ▶
Cooling by Evaporation
The temperature a wetted thermometer settles at
Blow unsaturated air past a wet wick and evaporation cools it to the wet-bulb temperature. The wet-bulb depression is a direct read on how dry the air is.
▸ THE THREE TEMPERATURES
Dry-bulb  T:     ordinary air temperature
Wet-bulb  T_wb:  evaporatively cooled (T_wb ≤ T)
Dew point T_dp:  T_dp ≤ T_wb ≤ T   (equal only at saturation)
▸ LEWIS RELATION (air–water)
For the air–water system, Le ≈ 1, so the adiabatic-saturation
temperature ≈ the thermodynamic wet-bulb temperature.
This lucky coincidence is why the psychrometric chart works so cleanly.
🥵 Human survivability tracks wet-bulb, not dry-bulb: a sustained T_wb above ~35 °C defeats sweat-based cooling regardless of shade or wind.
↔️ Air Mixing & Psychrometric Processes APPLIED ▶
Moving Around the Chart
Heating, cooling, humidifying & mixing as vectors
Every HVAC process is a path on the psychrometric chart. Recognising the direction each unit operation moves the air state makes system design intuitive.
▸ PROCESS DIRECTIONS
ProcessOn the chart
Sensible heatingMove right, W constant, RH falls
Sensible coolingMove left, W constant, RH rises
Cooling + dehumidifyDown-left below dew point (coil condenses water)
Evaporative coolingUp-left along a constant-wet-bulb line
▸ ADIABATIC MIXING
Mix two air streams → state lands on the straight line
between them, split by mass ratio (lever rule on W and h).
🧭 The "lever rule" for mixing is the same tie-line idea used for phase envelopes in Thermodynamics — geometry beats formulas here.
🍃 Drying of Solids APPLIED ▶
Psychrometrics Meets Unit Ops
Constant-rate and falling-rate drying periods
Industrial drying — of pharmaceuticals, food, polymers — is a coupled heat- and mass-transfer problem set by the humidity of the drying air.
▸ THE DRYING CURVE
Constant-rate period: free surface moisture; rate set by
   air (wet-bulb T at the surface) — evaporation-limited.
Falling-rate period:  internal diffusion controls; rate
   drops toward the equilibrium moisture content.
▸ DRYER TYPES
DryerBest for
Tray / batchSmall lots, fragile solids
Fluidized-bedFree-flowing granules, fast
Spray dryerSlurries → powder (milk, detergents)
Freeze dryer (lyophilization)Heat-sensitive biologics
💊 Equilibrium moisture content depends on the air's RH — you can never dry a solid below what the surrounding humidity allows.
