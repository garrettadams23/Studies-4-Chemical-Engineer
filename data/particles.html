⚪ Particle Characterization & Size Distribution FOUNDATION ▶
Describing a Powder
Size, shape and distribution govern behavior
Half of all chemical products ship as particles. How they flow, dissolve and react depends on a particle-size distribution, not a single size.
▸ MEAN DIAMETERS
Sauter mean D₃₂ = Σn d³ / Σn d²   (surface-volume — key for reaction/mass transfer)
Mass median D₅₀ = 50% of mass below this size
Span = (D₉₀ − D₁₀)/D₅₀   (width of the distribution)
▸ MEASUREMENT METHODS
MethodRange / basis
SievingCoarse, by mesh
Laser diffractionWide range, by scattering
Microscopy / imagingShape + size
🔬 The Sauter mean D₃₂ sets the surface area per volume — the size that matters most for dissolution, combustion and catalysis.
🌫️ Powder Flow, Storage & Fluidization HANDLING ▶
Solids Are Not Fluids
Why hoppers jam and beds bubble
Bulk solids behave unlike liquids — they build stress, arch and rat-hole. Understanding flow and fluidization prevents the notorious solids-handling failures.
▸ HOPPER FLOW PATTERNS
PatternBehavior
Mass flowAll solids move — first in, first out (preferred)
Funnel flowCentral channel; dead zones, segregation, rat-holing
▸ FLUIDIZATION
At minimum fluidization velocity u_mf, drag = weight:
  bed pressure drop ΔP = (1 − ε)(ρ_s − ρ_g) g L

Below u_mf: fixed bed.  Above: bubbling → slugging → transport.
Geldart groups (A/B/C/D) predict fluidization quality by size & density.
🏭 A well-designed steep, smooth hopper gives mass flow; the wrong wall angle causes arching and erratic feed — a classic, costly design miss.
🧱 Granulation & Agglomeration SIZE ENLARGEMENT ▶
Making Bigger Particles on Purpose
Turning fine powders into flowable granules
Fine powders dust, don't flow and segregate. Granulation binds them into larger granules that flow, dose and compress well — vital in pharma and fertilizers.
▸ WHY ENLARGE SIZE
BenefitReason
Better flowLarger particles are less cohesive
Less dustSafety & yield (dust explosions!)
Uniform blendPrevents segregation of a mix
▸ METHODS
Wet granulation:  binder liquid + mixing → dry (fluid bed)
Dry granulation:  roller compaction → mill to granules
Spray drying:     atomize solution → dry droplets into particles
💊 Tablet manufacturing is granulation → compression; granule quality controls tablet hardness, dissolution and content uniformity (see Pharma/GMP).
🌬️ Pneumatic Conveying & Solids Transport HANDLING ▶
Moving Powders in a Gas Stream
Blowing solids through pipes
Pneumatic conveying transports bulk solids in a moving gas stream through pipes — flexible routing and enclosed (dust-free) transfer, at the cost of energy and attrition.
▸ CONVEYING MODES
ModeCharacter
Dilute phaseHigh gas velocity, particles suspended — simple, erosive
Dense phaseLow velocity, slugs/plugs — gentle, less attrition
▸ DESIGN CONCERNS
Keep gas above the saltation velocity (or solids drop out & plug).
Bends erode (dilute phase) — use long-radius or blind tees.
Attrition & segregation degrade fragile or mixed powders.
⚠️ Moving powders generates static and dust — a dust-explosion and grounding concern (see Process Safety); inert-gas conveying is used for reactive solids.
