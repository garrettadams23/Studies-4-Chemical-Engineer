⚪ Particle Characterization & Size Distribution FOUNDATION ▶
Describing a Powder
Size, shape and distribution govern behavior
Half of all chemical products ship as particles. How they flow, dissolve and react depends on a particle-size distribution, not a single size.
▸ MEAN DIAMETERS
Sauter mean D₃₂ = Σn d³ / Σn d²   (surface-volume — key for reaction/mass transfer)
Mass median D₅₀ = 50% of mass below this size
Span = (D₉₀ − D₁₀)/D₅₀   (width of the distribution)
▸ MEASUREMENT METHODS
MethodRange / basis
SievingCoarse, by mesh
Laser diffractionWide range, by scattering
Microscopy / imagingShape + size
🔬 The Sauter mean D₃₂ sets the surface area per volume — the size that matters most for dissolution, combustion and catalysis.
🌫️ Powder Flow, Storage & Fluidization HANDLING ▶
Solids Are Not Fluids
Why hoppers jam and beds bubble
Bulk solids behave unlike liquids — they build stress, arch and rat-hole. Understanding flow and fluidization prevents the notorious solids-handling failures.
▸ HOPPER FLOW PATTERNS
PatternBehavior
Mass flowAll solids move — first in, first out (preferred)
Funnel flowCentral channel; dead zones, segregation, rat-holing
▸ FLUIDIZATION
At minimum fluidization velocity u_mf, drag = weight:
  bed pressure drop ΔP = (1 − ε)(ρ_s − ρ_g) g L

Below u_mf: fixed bed.  Above: bubbling → slugging → transport.
Geldart groups (A/B/C/D) predict fluidization quality by size & density.
🏭 A well-designed steep, smooth hopper gives mass flow; the wrong wall angle causes arching and erratic feed — a classic, costly design miss.
