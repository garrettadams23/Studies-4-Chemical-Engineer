Building Blocks of a Plant
Any process is a sequence of unit operations
The unit-operations concept (Arthur D. Little, 1915) recognizes that every process is assembled from a finite set of standard physical operations — the same distillation column serves oil refining and brewing alike.
▸ CLASSIFICATION
CategoryOperations
Fluid handlingPumps, compressors, fans, piping
Heat transferExchangers, reboilers, condensers, furnaces
Mass transferDistillation, absorption, extraction, drying
Mechanical separationFiltration, centrifugation, sedimentation, cyclones
Solids / mixingCrushing, crystallization, agitation, fluidization
🔧 Learn the operation once and you can apply it across every industry — the power of the unit-operations abstraction.
