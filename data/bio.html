🧬 DNA & the Central Dogma of Molecular Biology FOUNDATION ▶
The Molecule of Heredity
DNA → RNA → Protein — information flow in the cell
Deoxyribonucleic acid (DNA) is the double-helix polymer that stores genetic information. Chemical engineers harness this machinery in fermentation, recombinant-protein production and biofuels. The central dogma describes how the information is read.
▸ STRUCTURE — THE DOUBLE HELIX
FeatureDetail
BackboneAlternating sugar (deoxyribose) + phosphate
BasesA, T, G, C — the four-letter code
Base pairingA=T (2 H-bonds) · G≡C (3 H-bonds)
GeometryAntiparallel strands, ~10.5 bp per turn (Watson–Crick, 1953)
▸ THE CENTRAL DOGMA

   DNA ───────────► DNA        (cell division)
    │
    │ transcription
    ▼
   RNA (mRNA)
    │
    │ translation  (ribosome, codons → amino acids)

Template : 5'- A  T  G  C  A  G -3'

🧪 Recombinant DNA technology inserts a target gene into a host (e.g. E. coli) so an engineered bioreactor mass-produces the protein — how insulin is made industrially.
🔑 Enzyme Kinetics — Michaelis–Menten BIOCATALYSIS ▶
Biological Catalysts
Saturable rate laws for enzyme reactions
Enzymes are protein catalysts that lower activation energy enormously. Their rate saturates with substrate — the hallmark of the Michaelis–Menten model.
▸ MICHAELIS–MENTEN RATE LAW
        V_max · [S]
  v  = ───────────────
          K_m + [S]

V_max = max rate (all enzyme saturated)
K_m   = substrate conc. at v = V_max/2
        (↓ K_m ⇒ tighter enzyme–substrate binding)
▸ LIMITING BEHAVIOR
RegimeRate lawOrder in [S]
[S] ≪ K_mv ≈ (V_max/K_m)[S]First order
[S] ≫ K_mv ≈ V_maxZero order
📉 A Lineweaver–Burk plot (1/v vs 1/[S]) linearizes the model — intercept = 1/V_max, slope = K_m/V_max.
🦠 Fermentation & Bioreactor Design SCALE-UP ▶
Cells as Chemical Plants
Growing microbes to make product
A bioreactor is a reactor whose catalyst is alive. Cell growth, substrate uptake and product formation are coupled, and oxygen transfer usually limits scale-up.
▸ CELL-GROWTH KINETICS (MONOD)
          μ_max · S
  μ  = ───────────────      (specific growth rate)
           K_s + S

dX/dt = μ X            (exponential growth)
Y_X/S = biomass yield per substrate consumed
▸ OPERATING MODES
ModeFeature
BatchFixed nutrients; simple, lower productivity
Fed-batchFeed added over time; avoids substrate inhibition
Continuous (chemostat)Steady state; μ = dilution rate D
▸ OXYGEN TRANSFER — THE SCALE-UP BOTTLENECK
OTR = k_L a · (C* − C_L)

k_L a = mass-transfer coefficient (agitation, sparging)
C*    = saturation O₂;  C_L = dissolved O₂ in broth
💨 Because O₂ is sparingly soluble, kLa is the number most fought over when scaling a fermenter from the bench to 100 m³.
⚗️ Downstream Processing & Purification RECOVERY ▶
From Broth to Product
Separating a fragile molecule from a dilute soup
Making the product is half the job; recovering it — often at high purity from a very dilute, heat-sensitive broth — can dominate the cost of a biopharmaceutical.
▸ TYPICAL PURIFICATION TRAIN
Broth
  │  1. Cell separation   (centrifuge / microfiltration)
  ▼
  │  2. Cell disruption   (if product is intracellular)
  ▼
  │  3. Concentration     (ultrafiltration)
  ▼
  │  4. Purification      (chromatography — affinity, ion-exchange)
  ▼
  │  5. Polishing / formulation  (diafiltration, lyophilization)
  ▼

🧫 Chromatography exploits the same molecular-affinity principle as the extraction and adsorption operations in the Mass Transfer domain — biology just makes it exquisitely selective.
✂️ Recombinant DNA — Cloning, PCR & CRISPR GENETIC ENGINEERING ▶
Reprogramming Cells to Make Product
The toolkit behind industrial biotech
Recombinant DNA technology inserts a gene of interest into a host cell so it becomes a living factory — how insulin, growth hormone and many enzymes are produced.
▸ THE CLONING WORKFLOW
1. Cut DNA at specific sites  → restriction enzymes
2. Insert gene into a vector  → plasmid + DNA ligase
3. Transform into a host      → E. coli / yeast / CHO cells
4. Select & screen          → antibiotic markers
5. Express & harvest         → the recombinant protein
▸ KEY TOOLS
ToolDoes
PCRAmplifies a target sequence millions-fold
Gel electrophoresisSeparates DNA by size (charge/sieving)
Restriction enzymesCut DNA at specific recognition sites
CRISPR-Cas9Precise, programmable genome editing
🧬 CHO (Chinese hamster ovary) cells are the workhorse host for complex human therapeutic proteins — they fold and glycosylate like human cells.
🌡️ Sterilization & Thermal Death Kinetics CONTAMINATION CONTROL ▶
Keeping the Culture Pure
Killing contaminants follows first-order kinetics
A single stray microbe can ruin a batch. Sterilizing media and equipment relies on first-order thermal death kinetics — and the same Arrhenius temperature dependence as any reaction.
▸ THE DEL FACTOR
dN/dt = −k_d N        (first-order death)

Del factor  ∇ = ln(N_0 / N) = ∫ k_d dt

Design target: ∇ ≈ 30–45 (N goes from ~10^12 down to <1)
k_d rises steeply with T (Arrhenius) → hotter, shorter is better.
▸ STERILIZATION ROUTES
MethodUse
Moist heat (autoclave)Media, equipment (121 °C steam)
Filtration (0.2 µm)Heat-sensitive media, air, feed gas
Continuous (HTST)Large-scale media — high T, short time
🔥 Because death is faster-accelerating with T than nutrient destruction, HTST (high-temperature short-time) sterilizes with less damage to the medium.
🕸️ Metabolic Engineering & Flux Balance CELL FACTORIES ▶
Rewiring Metabolism
Redirecting cellular fluxes toward a product
Metabolic engineering redesigns a cell's reaction network to overproduce a target — and flux balance analysis is the material balance that predicts what's possible.
▸ FLUX BALANCE ANALYSIS (FBA)
At steady state, every internal metabolite balances:
  S · v = 0     (S = stoichiometric matrix, v = flux vector)

Underdetermined → optimize an objective (usually growth or product)
subject to flux bounds → linear program (see Eng Math optimization).
▸ ENGINEERING LEVERS
LeverAction
OverexpressBoost a rate-limiting enzyme
Knock outDelete competing pathways
IntroduceAdd a heterologous pathway (new product)
🧬 FBA is a material balance on a metabolic network solved as an LP — chemical-engineering tools (balances + optimization) applied inside the cell.
💉 Biologics — Antibodies & Vaccines THERAPEUTICS ▶
Medicine Made by Cells
Large molecules that fight disease
Biologics — monoclonal antibodies, vaccines, gene therapies — are large, complex molecules made in living cells. Producing them is bioprocess engineering at its most demanding.

→ polish (ion-exchange) → viral clearance → formulate

Purity, glycosylation & viral safety are paramount.

🫀 Tissue Engineering & Regenerative Medicine FRONTIER ▶
Growing Living Tissue
Cells + scaffold + signals + transport
Tissue engineering builds living replacements for damaged tissue. Getting nutrients and oxygen into growing 3-D tissue is a classic transport problem at the cell scale.
▸ THE THREE INGREDIENTS
ElementRole
CellsBuild the tissue (often stem cells)
Scaffold3-D template & mechanical support
Signals / bioreactorGrowth factors + controlled environment
▸ THE TRANSPORT LIMIT
O₂ diffuses only ~100–200 µm into tissue before cells starve.
Thick tissue needs vascularization or perfusion bioreactors
— a diffusion-with-consumption problem (Transport + Reaction).
🔬 The oxygen-diffusion limit is the same shell-balance physics as a catalyst pellet — a striking cross-over between Transport Phenomena and biology.
