🧪 Acids, Bases & pH EQUILIBRIA ▶
Proton Transfer
Controlling pH controls the process
Acid–base equilibria govern reaction rates, corrosion, precipitation and biological activity. pH is one of the most-measured variables in any plant.
▸ pH & EQUILIBRIUM
pH = −log₁₀[H⁺]        pH + pOH = 14  (25 °C)

Ka = [H⁺][A⁻]/[HA]     pKa = −log Ka
Henderson–Hasselbalch: pH = pKa + log([A⁻]/[HA])
▸ STRENGTH SCALE
pHNatureExample
0–3Strong acidHCl, H₂SO₄
7NeutralPure water
11–14Strong baseNaOH, KOH
🧫 A buffer (weak acid + conjugate base) resists pH change — essential in fermentation and biopharmaceutical processing.
