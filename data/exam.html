🎓 The FE Chemical Exam FIRST STEP ▶
Fundamentals of Engineering
The first milestone toward licensure
The NCEES FE Chemical is a computer-based exam usually taken near graduation — the gateway to Engineer-in-Training status.
▸ FORMAT & HIGH-WEIGHT AREAS
110 questions · 6 hours · closed-book (NCEES reference handbook provided)

Heaviest topics: Material/Energy Balances, Thermodynamics,
Heat/Mass Transfer, Fluids, Reaction Engineering, Math/Probability.
▸ STRATEGY
TipWhy
Learn the handbookEvery equation is in it — know where
Watch unitsMost errors are unit slips
Pace ~1.9 min/questionFlag & return to hard ones

📜 The PE Exam & Path to Licensure LICENSURE ▶
Becoming a Professional Engineer
FE → experience → PE
The PE (Professional Engineer) license lets you take legal responsibility for engineering work. It follows the FE plus qualifying experience.
▸ THE PATH
1. Graduate (ABET-accredited program)
2. Pass the FE → become an EIT / EI
3. Gain ~4 years of qualifying experience under a PE
4. Pass the PE Chemical exam
5. Apply to your state board → PE license (renew with CEUs)
▸ WHY IT MATTERS
PE unlocks
Sign & sealLegally certify designs/reports
ConsultingOffer services to the public
AdvancementRequired for many senior roles
⚖️ With the seal comes the paramount duty (see Process Safety & Ethics): public safety over client or employer interest.
