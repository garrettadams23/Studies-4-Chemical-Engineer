📜 The PE Exam & Path to Licensure LICENSURE ▶
Becoming a Professional Engineer
FE → experience → PE
The PE (Professional Engineer) license lets you take legal responsibility for engineering work. It follows the FE plus qualifying experience.
▸ THE PATH
1. Graduate (ABET-accredited program)
2. Pass the FE → become an EIT / EI
3. Gain ~4 years of qualifying experience under a PE
4. Pass the PE Chemical exam
5. Apply to your state board → PE license (renew with CEUs)
▸ WHY IT MATTERS
PE unlocks
Sign & sealLegally certify designs/reports
ConsultingOffer services to the public
AdvancementRequired for many senior roles
⚖️ With the seal comes the paramount duty (see Process Safety & Ethics): public safety over client or employer interest.

✏️ Practice-Question Walkthrough PRACTICE ▶
Think Like the Exam
A worked multiple-choice example
Exam questions reward a fast, unit-careful method. Here's the mindset on a typical item — and try the interactive Self-Test quiz for more.
▸ SAMPLE & METHOD
Q: Water (μ=0.001 Pa·s, ρ=1000) flows at 1 m/s in a 0.1 m pipe.
   Is the flow laminar or turbulent?

1. Re = ρvD/μ = 1000·1·0.1 / 0.001 = 100,000
2. Re ≫ 4000 → TURBULENT.
3. Decoy answers give 100 or 1e8 (unit slips) — check magnitude!

Verify with the Reynolds calculator in the Tools domain.
🎯 Recognize the concept (regime → Reynolds), plug in with consistent units, and sanity- check the exponent. Most wrong options are unit-error decoys.
